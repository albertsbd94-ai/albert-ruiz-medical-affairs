The Future of Medical Affairs: five shifts underway

Albert Ruiz de la Oliva · 7 min read

Medical Affairs isn't being disrupted so much as it's maturing — into a function that's more digital, more data-informed and more augmented by AI, without losing the scientific discipline that gives it credibility. Five shifts stand out.

1. AI as a genuine productivity layer

Literature triage, meeting preparation and insight structuring are shifting from manual to AI-assisted — always with human validation. See AI in Medical Affairs.

2. Omnichannel becomes the default, not the exception

Scientific engagement now spans in-person, virtual, digital content and asynchronous formats — coordinated as one narrative rather than separate channels. See Digital Medical Affairs.

3. Digital Opinion Leaders join traditional KOLs

Scientific influence increasingly lives online too — and identifying it responsibly is becoming its own discipline. See Digital Opinion Leaders.

4. Data-informed prioritisation

Engagement and insight data increasingly help teams decide where scientific exchange creates the most value — without replacing scientific judgement about where it's needed.

5. A broader Future MSL skill set

Digital fluency, AI literacy and data comfort are becoming baseline skills alongside deep scientific expertise — not a replacement for it.

What doesn't change

Evidence before opinion. Human connection over automation for anything that matters. That's the constant underneath every one of these shifts.
