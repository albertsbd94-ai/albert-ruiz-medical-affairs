Who it's for

Built for people who work at the interface of science and the field

MSLs entering the field

Life-science or clinical professionals moving into a Medical Science Liaison role who want a structured foundation instead of learning purely on the job.

Practicing MSLs

Current MSLs who want to sharpen KOL/DOL engagement, insight quality and scientific-exchange skills, and prepare for the next step in Medical Affairs.

Medical Affairs teams

Curriculum

12 modules, start to finish

Every module includes lessons, worked examples, a case study, curated resources and a graded quiz.

Module 01

Medical Affairs Foundations & Strategic Role

The strategic role of Medical Affairs as an independent, science-first function connecting clinical practice and business strategy.

Module 02

The Modern MSL Role

The responsibilities, competencies and mindset of the modern MSL as a strategic medical partner in their territory.

Module 03

KOL Mapping, Segmentation & Engagement

Identifying, segmenting, prioritising and building sustainable scientific relationships with KOLs and DOLs.

Module 04

Scientific Exchange & High-Impact Conversations

High-value scientific conversations with healthcare professionals, grounded in genuine exploration rather than argumentation.

Module 05

Medical Insights Excellence

Identifying, capturing, analysing and transforming medical insights into actionable strategic decisions.

Module 06

Evidence-Based Medicine for MSLs

Critical interpretation of scientific evidence relevant to Medical Affairs practice.

Module 07

Clinical Research, RWE & Evidence Generation

How Medical Affairs actively contributes to evidence generation beyond regulatory clinical development.

Module 08

Congress Excellence

Preparing, executing and capitalising on scientific congresses from a Medical Affairs perspective.

Module 09

Medical Strategy & Medical Plan

How a solid medical strategy is built and translated into an executable, measurable Medical Plan.

Module 10

Omnichannel Medical & Digital Opinion Leaders

The new digital environment of Medical Affairs, integrating in-person and digital channels coherently.

Module 11

AI for Medical Affairs

Using artificial intelligence strategically, ethically and practically — without compromising compliance or scientific rigor.

Module 12

Medical Affairs Leadership & Career Accelerator

Developing medical leadership, professional brand and a long-term career strategy in Medical Affairs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the semi-presential and the fully online modality?

Both modalities give full access to the same 12 modules, lessons, quizzes and certificate. The semi-presential modality additionally includes live mentoring sessions with Albert — exact dates are confirmed by email after enrolment.

Do I need previous Medical Affairs experience?

No. The course starts from Medical Affairs foundations and builds up to advanced topics like medical strategy and AI, so it works both for people entering the field and for practicing MSLs who want to structure and deepen their expertise.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — a certificate of completion is issued once all 12 modules and their quizzes are completed.

How do I access the course after purchase?

After a successful payment you'll receive an email with your campus access instructions, typically within 24-48 business hours.

Can I pay in instalments, or enrol a team?

For instalments, team or company enrolment, contact Albert directly before purchasing.

I have a question before enrolling — can I ask first?

Yes — reach out before you buy if you're unsure which modality fits you best.
