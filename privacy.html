Privacy Policy

This site is a personal website. It collects the minimum data needed to operate, and never sells or shares it with third parties for advertising.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to "The Future of Medical Affairs" newsletter, your email address is stored solely to send you newsletter issues and community updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email, or by writing to hello@albertruizdelaoliva.com.

Contact form

The "Work With Me" contact form opens your own email client with a pre-filled message — no message data passes through or is stored by this website itself.

Cookies & analytics

This site aims to use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics limited to aggregate, anonymised traffic patterns (e.g. page views) — never used to build an individual profile or shared with advertisers.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data held (such as a newsletter subscription) by writing to hello@albertruizdelaoliva.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site evolves. The date of the most recent update will always be reflected here.
